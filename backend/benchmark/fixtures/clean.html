Fresh Sourdough, Pastries, and Coffee in Downtown Rivertown

Our Story

Riverside Bakery has been baking fresh sourdough and pastries in downtown Rivertown for over a decade. Every loaf is proofed for twenty-four hours and baked fresh each morning, using a starter that has been maintained since the bakery first opened its doors.

We source our flour from a local mill and our coffee from a small-batch roaster two towns over, so every visit supports other small businesses in the region alongside our own.

Visit Us

We're open daily from 7am to 4pm at 12 River Street. Walk-ins are always welcome, and we also take custom cake and pastry orders for events with at least 48 hours notice.
